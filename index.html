Will it melt in delivery?

From March to October we ship in insulated boxes with gel packs, and we only dispatch from Monday to Wednesday so nothing waits in a warehouse over a weekend.

Are the dark bars vegan?

Velnox, Twylune and Crestide contain only cocoa beans, cocoa butter, cane sugar and, in Crestide, sea salt. They are made on equipment that also handles milk, so we don't label them vegan.

How long does a bar keep?

Twelve months in a cool, dry cupboard away from strong smells. Keep it out of the fridge; the moisture dulls the snap.

Can I order for a wedding or an office?

Yes. We engrave names, print custom sleeves and ship to multiple addresses. Write to us with your date and numbers.
